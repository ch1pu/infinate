## Executive Summary

INFINATE's M1.11 Strafe Jumping Navigation represents a breakthrough in AI context handling that enables practical applications previously impossible due to computational constraints. As an **open-source project**, INFINATE empowers developers, researchers, and organizations worldwide to build AI systems with unlimited context.

### The Core Problem We Solve

**Traditional AI models have a fatal flaw:** They can only "remember" a limited amount of context (typically 4K-128K tokens) before running out of memory or becoming prohibitively slow.

**INFINATE solves this** by organizing AI memory spatially (like a 3D map) instead of linearly (like a list), achieving:

- **10,317× faster** than MIT's best alternative
- **1,330× cheaper** per query
- **Unlimited context** (billions of tokens possible)
- **O(k) constant complexity** (speed doesn't degrade with more data)
- **100% open source** (free to use, modify, and distribute)
